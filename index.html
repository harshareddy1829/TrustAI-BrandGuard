How it works

From draft to confident approval.

Five stages, in order. The system recommends, and a person decides.

1. Import

Add brand guidelines, approved facts, terminology and reusable assets.

2. Upload

Submit text, images, advertisements or documents for review.

3. Analyse

Check tone, claims, facts, visuals, messaging and accessibility against your rules.

4. Improve

Each issue is explained, with a suggested correction and its supporting reference.

5. Approve

A reviewer approves the content or requests changes.

AI recommends. Humans decide.
